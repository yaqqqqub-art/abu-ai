Getting Abu AI running

Four steps, once — after that it's just opening a browser tab on your phone.

Unzip and open Abu AI Desktop Double-click Abu AI Desktop.exe to launch it — see README.txt inside the download if you'd rather run it from source.
Create your Username + Password Asked once, the first time it runs — typed straight into the terminal window.
Find your PC's IP address Windows: ipconfig → look for "IPv4 Address"
Open it on your phone Same Wi-Fi network, then visit http://<pc-ip>:5000 and log in.

⚠ Local Wi-Fi only — anyone with a valid login on your network can control this PC. Don't expose it to the open internet.
